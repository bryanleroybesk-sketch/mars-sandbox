RD Global Connect

Power, connections, innovation. An operating company with technology built in.

Company

Registered in Chon Buri, working in three markets.

RD Global Connect Co., Ltd. is registered in Nong Prue, Bang Lamung District, Chon Buri Province, Thailand. We are the operating partner on the ground for Experience Pattaya City.

Our services cover destination management, ground logistics, event and film production, vendor and venue contracts and government liaison with TCEB, TAT and DASTA. We also build applications, including WAWA, CLO, Experience Pattaya City and the Pulverlaguer app.

We work for partners in Thailand and plan to Swiss expectations of precision, reliability and accountability, with a network that reaches Dubai.

Leadership

One team, led from Chon Buri.

RD Global Connect works alongside BESK Creative, its sister company. Together they combine operations and creative technology.
